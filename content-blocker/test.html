Content blocker $third-party in cross-site frames

The top frame and a cross-site iframe each load script.js twice with a <script> tag: once from their own domain (first party) and once from the other domain (third party). Both domains have a $third-party rule for their script.js, so in every frame the first-party load should succeed and the third-party load should be blocked.

Custom filters required:





  
    

      Frame
      First party (own domain)
expected: loaded		
      Third party (other domain)
expected: blocked		
    

  
  





  If the iframe row is reversed (first party blocked, third party loaded), the
  content blocker is judging the iframe's requests against the top-level page
  instead of the iframe.
